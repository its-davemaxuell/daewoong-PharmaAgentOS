the official free subset of [Streamline Light](https://www.streamlinehq.com/icons/streamline-light).
Retrieved 10 September 2026 through Streamline's public free collection endpoint.
Each selected asset was marked `isFree: true` and `familySlug: ultimate-light-free`.
The collection explicitly identifies its SVGs as CC BY 4.0.

`manifest.json` records original names, hashes, source URLs and adaptations.
`sources/` retains the original SVGs. The React output is generated locally with
no external icon requests, icon font, runtime parser or third-party icon engine.

Modifications: color inherits the application's semantic state; stroke weight is
normalized to 1.25 on a 24-unit grid for small controls. Directional variants rotate
the original geometry. The double chevron is reduced to one centered chevron;
search, close, subtract, message and check icons omit their enclosing shape;
the plus rotates the unframed close strokes; status markers reuse the source
circle with an optional center dot. No regulatory or organizational logo is altered.

to these icon assets independently of the surrounding application's license.
